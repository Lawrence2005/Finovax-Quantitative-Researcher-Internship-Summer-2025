## 🎯 Project Background and Significance
In the realm of options trading, having robust backtesting frameworks is essential for evaluating the performance of various trading strategies. Python offers several libraries designed for backtesting, but it's crucial to identify which ones are best suited for options trading specifically. The goal is to search for and **evaluate Python-based backtesting frameworks that can effectively handle options trading**, including Backtrader, a well-known backtesting library. Choosing the right backtesting framework will **enable the team to rigorously test and refine options trading strategies**, ultimately leading to more informed trading decisions and improved performance.

## 📋 Project Requirements
**1. Search**
- Compile a list of at least three solid Python-based backtesting frameworks, including Backtrader, that support options trading.

**2. Evaluate**
- For each framework, evaluate the following aspects:
    - Ease of use and documentation quality
    - Support for options backtesting, including data handling and simulated executions of options strategies
    - Flexibility and customization options
    - Community support and ongoing maintenance

**3. Analyze**
- Analyze in detail why each framework is good or not suitable, including specific features of strengths and limitations.

## 📦 Deliverable
A PDF report of at least 5 pages, formatted in 12-pt Times New Roman and double-spaced, that contains all the findings with screenshots, examples, or snippets of code where applicable.
